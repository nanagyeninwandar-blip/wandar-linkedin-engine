# 360Brew — LinkedIn Algorithm Intelligence (May 2026)

## What 360Brew is

LinkedIn deployed 360Brew on March 12, 2026. It is a 150-billion-parameter AI foundation model that replaced LinkedIn's previous fragmented recommendation systems. Built on Meta's LLaMA 3 architecture, trained exclusively on LinkedIn's proprietary data.

The critical shift: the old algorithm rewarded people who were good at LinkedIn. 360Brew rewards people who are good at their craft and can communicate that clearly.

**Median post impressions are down 47% year-on-year** (from 1,211 to 636). Average reach has fallen to 8-12% of followers, from 15-20% a year ago. This is not a crisis — it is a precision filter. Experts with relevant audiences still grow.

---

## How 360Brew ranks content

**Two-stage process:**

1. **Retrieval** — A Causal LLM converts posts and profiles into semantic vectors. Narrows millions of posts to ~2,000 candidates using cosine similarity matching (semantic relevance, not keyword matching).

2. **Ranking** — A Generative Recommender analyzes the viewer's last 1,000+ interactions as a chronological sequence. It predicts where their interests are heading, not just what they engaged with historically.

The algorithm reads meaning, not metrics.

## Primary ranking signals (what 360Brew rewards)

| Signal | Weight | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Topical consistency | High | 3-4 editorial pillars. Drift kills reach. |
| Original expertise | High | First-party data, novel perspectives. AI-generated content is detected and suppressed. |
| Substantive comments | High | Comments weighted far more than likes. Multi-sentence comments > single words. |
| Saves | High | 5x more reach than a like. Strongest utility signal. |
| Dwell time | High | How long someone reads before scrolling. 30 seconds of reading outperforms 50 quick likes. |
| Profile-content alignment | Medium | Semantic fingerprint between profile and posts must be coherent. |
| Early engagement (first 60 min) | Critical | Posts shown to 2-5% of network first. That window determines distribution. |

## What 360Brew penalizes

- Generic AI-generated content (detectable)
- Engagement pods and coordinated activity rings
- Mechanical engagement bait ("Comment YES if...")
- Keyword stuffing and clickbait formats
- Editorial inconsistency (topic drift)
- Daily posting (-45% reach vs. 2-3x/week)
- External links in post body (~60% reach reduction)
- "Link in first comment" workaround — also now penalized as of early 2026

## News Content Mechanics

**Claim:** "LinkedIn rewards news and journalist content"

**Reality:** 360Brew does NOT have an explicit "news boost" or "journalist bonus."

**However, news content naturally triggers three high-weight 360Brew signals:**

1. **Dwell time** — Substantive reporting/analysis keeps readers engaged 30+ seconds
2. **Substantive comments** — News sparks multi-sentence debate (high algorithmic weight)
3. **Original expertise** — First-party reporting or insider analysis signals original content

**The mechanism:**
- News posts that provide analysis (not just aggregation) increase dwell time
- Breaking industry news triggers debate → substantive comments
- First-to-report or unique angle → original expertise signal

**Strategic implication for Wandar:**
- News-first content (newsjacking <7 days fresh) can amplify reach IF paired with original safari operator insight
- Aggregating news without analysis = low value
- Wandar angle: "Here's the news + why it matters to safari operators" = high value

**Timing windows for news content:**
- 0-48 hours: Breaking news window (highest freshness score)
- 3-7 days: Newsjacking window (still fresh, analysis appreciated)
- 8-14 days: Analysis window (not newsjacking, but depth matters)
- 15+ days: Evergreen only (news angle expired)

## Hashtag guidance

- Posts with more than 3 hashtags: 70% lower reach
- 2-3 relevant hashtags: +85% impressions vs. no hashtags
- Zero hashtags: neutral

Use 2-3 semantically relevant hashtags per post. Never stuff.
