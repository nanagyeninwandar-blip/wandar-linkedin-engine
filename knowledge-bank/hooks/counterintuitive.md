# Counterintuitive Hooks

These hooks work by stating the opposite of what most people expect. The reader's brain flags the contradiction and forces a pause.

**Rule:** The counterintuitive claim must be true and provable in the post body. Never bait and switch.

---

## Proven patterns (from 0B-linkedin-creator.md)

**The hidden leader:**
"The best safari camp in Kenya has 47 TripAdvisor reviews. Their website has never been updated."

Why it works: Names a credible signal (TripAdvisor), pairs it with an expected failure (outdated website), creates a mystery.

---

**The wasted spend:**
"Safari operators spend $3,000/month on Instagram ads targeting people who book through travel advisors."

Why it works: Specific dollar amount + the irony of targeting the wrong channel = instant operator recognition.

---

**The language gap:**
"Your guests say one thing about your safari. Your website says something completely different. That gap is costing you bookings."

Why it works: Every operator who reads this immediately thinks of their own site. Personal without being personal.

---

**The invisible competitor:**
"Most safari operators aren't losing bookings to competitors. They're losing them to silence."

Why it works: Reframes the competitive problem entirely. No operator expects this framing.

---

## Construction formula

1. State the outcome everyone expects
2. Flip it with the real outcome
3. Keep it to one sentence

Bad: "You might think social media is the best way to find safari clients, but actually it isn't because..."
Good: "Social media isn't finding your safari clients. The forums are."
