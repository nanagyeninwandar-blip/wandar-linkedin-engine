# Direct Truth Hooks

These hooks state what everyone in the industry knows but nobody says out loud. The bluntness is the point.

**Rule:** Must be true. Must be uncomfortable. Must be specific to the safari/operator world — not generic advice.

---

## Proven patterns (from 0B-linkedin-creator.md)

**The website truth:**
"Most safari websites are beautiful and useless."

Why it works: Every operator has heard this concern but never seen it stated this baldly. Five words.

---

**The discovery truth:**
"Safari operators don't have a marketing problem. They have a visibility problem."

Why it works: Reframes the problem category. Operators have spent money on marketing. This tells them they were solving the wrong thing.

---

**The first response truth:**
"The first credible, knowledgeable response in a forum thread typically wins the relationship."

Why it works: States the entire competitive dynamic in one sentence. Operators either recognize this or are learning it for the first time.

---

**The referral truth:**
"Referrals alone are no longer enough."

Why it works: Four words. Every operator who depends on referrals feels this. Few say it out loud.

---

## Construction formula

1. Identify the thing everyone knows but doesn't say
2. Strip every qualifier: no "often", "sometimes", "in many cases"
3. State it as fact
4. Keep it under 15 words

Bad: "It can sometimes be difficult for safari operators to maintain visibility on digital platforms."
Good: "Safari operators are invisible at the moment travelers start planning."

---

## Edge handling

Some truths will land differently depending on the reader. Balance with:
- Follow-up sentence that acknowledges complexity OR
- Body that shows the path forward (not just the diagnosis)

Bluntness without direction reads as cynicism. Bluntness with direction reads as authority.
