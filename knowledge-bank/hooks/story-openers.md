# Story Opener Hooks

These hooks place the reader inside a specific moment. The specificity signals authenticity and stops the scroll.

**Rule:** The detail must be real. Never fabricate. Anonymize camps and operators, but keep the detail.

---

## Proven patterns (from 0B-linkedin-creator.md)

**The operator observation:**
"A walking safari operator in Laikipia told me something that changed how I think about luxury marketing."

Why it works: Specific region (Laikipia), specific niche (walking safari), implied revelation.

---

**The named guide:**
"23 of their last 30 TripAdvisor reviews mentioned their head guide by name. He's nowhere on their website."

Why it works: Specific numbers + specific platform + specific gap = undeniable observation.

---

**The conversation that shifted something:**
"I was on a call with an operator in the Selous last week. She said: 'We have 94% repeat guests. We have no idea why they keep coming back.'"

Why it works: Real dialogue, specific location, paradox (success without understanding).

---

## Construction formula

1. Name a real place (Laikipia, Selous, Bwindi, South Luangwa, Maasai Mara)
2. Name a real role (owner-operator, head guide, camp manager)
3. State what happened or what they said
4. The revelation comes in the body

Bad: "Someone told me something interesting about marketing."
Good: "A camp owner in Zambia showed me her booking inquiry inbox. Twelve messages. Two replies."

| Laikipia, Kenya | Private conservancy, walking-focused, community-led |
