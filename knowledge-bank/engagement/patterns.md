## Known winning patterns (seed intelligence)

### Hook type performance (from strategy docs)

| Hook type | Expected performance | Reason |
|-----------|---------------------|--------|
| Counterintuitive | High saves, high reposts | Challenges assumptions — people save to remember, repost to share the challenge |
| Story opener (specific place + person) | High comments, high operator replies | Operators recognize themselves in the story |
| Direct truth | High Growth reach, high reposts | Controversial enough to share, true enough to agree with |
| Number hook | High saves, moderate comments | People bookmark data; less likely to debate it |

### Format performance (from strategy docs)

| Format | Best metric | Worst metric | Notes |
|--------|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Insight (150-250 words) | Saves, relevant impressions | Comments | Strong for Authority bucket |
| Story | Comments, operator DMs | Saves | Best for Wednesday posting |
| List (3-5 items) | Saves, shares | Comments | Must be specific — generic lists get ignored |
| Framework | Saves, reposts | Comments | Best Friday post — people share frameworks |
| Contrast | Reposts, reach | Saves | "What everyone thinks vs. what's true" |

### Closing question performance

| Question type | Expected comments | Notes |
|---------------|------------------|-------|
| Specific operator question ("If you run a camp in X...") | High quality, low volume | Gets real operators to respond |
| Open industry question ("What do you see in your...") | Medium quality, medium volume | Invites peer conversation |
| Reflection question ("When did you last...") | Medium quality, medium volume | Good for personal bucket |
| No question — weighted statement close | Low comments, high saves | Best for framework posts |
