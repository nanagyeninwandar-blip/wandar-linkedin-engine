**Source signal:** AI agents are now executing luxury travel bookings. 12% of luxury travel bookings in Q1 2026. Projected 25% by Q3 2026. AI agents evaluate operators on structured data: pricing clarity, cancellation policy, amenity databases. Not storytelling. Not photography.
**Hook direction:** Counterintuitive — "Safari operators spent years perfecting their story. The next buyer might be an algorithm."
**Narrative arc:**
- Opening tension: AI agents are booking luxury travel. Not helping humans book — making the booking. They evaluate operators the same way a procurement system evaluates a vendor: structured data, pricing transparency, cancellation policy, availability.

This isn't a distant future problem.
