# Step 8: Learning Loop

After each completed task, pause and assess.
Do not skip this. Judgment improves through reflection.

---

**What felt real and strong?**

- Lines that landed without hedging
- Sections where the operator voice came through clearly
- Moments where specificity made the piece feel true
- Hooks that pulled without trying too hard

---

**What felt average?**

- Sections that were hard to make specific
- Lines that required multiple rewrites to remove AI patterns
- Places where the brief was weak and the output suffered for it
- Moments where polish masked a weak idea instead of fixing it

---

**The standard:**

Would a safari operator read this and feel understood?
Would they share it?

If yes to both: it passed.
If no to either: note what was missing. Apply it next time.
