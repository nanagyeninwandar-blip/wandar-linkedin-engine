# Step 3: Diagnose

## Channel Calibration (run before the checklist)

Before running the diagnostic checklist, lock in the channel context.

**Identify:**
1. What channel is this for?
2. Who is the specific audience?
3. What does success look like on this channel?
4. What are the failure modes specific to this channel?

Reference `knowledge/09_channel_intelligence.md`.

Apply the channel lens throughout diagnosis and rewriting.
Do not judge a LinkedIn post using landing page logic.
Do not judge a Medium article using newsletter logic.

**If this is a LinkedIn post:**
Identify the strategy before running the checklist.
Reference `knowledge/08_linkedin_strategy.md`.

Ask:
- Is this Brandjacking, Namejacking, Newsjacking, or a Hot take?
- Is the hook structured correctly for that strategy?
- Does the post body execute the strategy or abandon it mid-way?
- Are there technical failures (external link in body, dense paragraphs, no line breaks)?
- Is the audience correct for this strategy direction?

A LinkedIn post that is well-written but structured for the wrong strategy, or strong in content but technically wrong for the algorithm, fails. Catch both.

---

## Diagnostic Checklist (run after channel calibration)

- [ ] Generic? Could this apply to any industry?
- [ ] Forgettable? Would the specific audience for this piece skim past it?
- [ ] AI-sounding? Hedged phrasing, filler structure, pattern-matching language?
- [ ] Operator-blind? Misses the real world — referrals, WhatsApp, long booking cycles, trust-based decisions? (Only apply when operators are the audience.)
- [ ] Distribution-weak? No shareable line, no hook, no natural search phrase?
- [ ] Channel-misaligned? Formatted or structured in a way that contradicts how this channel serves and rewards content?

**Escalation:**

| Flags | Level |
|-------|-------|
| 1 | Light edit |
| 2 | Medium rewrite |
| 3+ | Heavy rewrite or full reconstruction |
| Fundamentally broken | Rejection Mode |

Do not skip this step.
Diagnosis determines the correct level of intervention.
Jumping to rewrite without diagnosis leads to over-editing strong content or under-editing weak content.
