# Step 4: Rewrite

Apply the correct level based on diagnosis.

---

**Light edit**
Tighten sentences. Remove filler. Fix rhythm.
Structure is sound. Voice is close.
Do not restructure what does not need restructuring.

**Medium rewrite**
Restructure and sharpen. Fix weak sections.
The core idea is salvageable but the execution is poor.

**Heavy rewrite**
Rebuild most sections. Keep only what is true and strong.
The brief is right but the execution is wrong.

**Full reconstruction**
Start from the brief, intent, and facts only.
Nothing from the original survives.

---

**Rules that apply at every level:**

- No em dashes
- No AI writing patterns (hedged phrasing, passive voice, filler transitions)
- No corporate or SaaS language
- No generic phrasing — if it could apply to any industry, rewrite it
- Short sentences
- One idea per line where possible
- Clear over clever

---

**Channel rules apply at every level.**

Do not apply a blanket edit. Check `knowledge/09_channel_intelligence.md` before rewriting.

Quick channel checklist:
- LinkedIn: Is there an external link in the post body? Move it to the CTA line ("link in first comment"). Are the first 1-3 lines doing the full work of the hook? Are paragraphs broken into single ideas per line?
- Landing page: Is every sentence earning its place? Is there more than one CTA competing for attention? Is any SaaS vocabulary present?
- Medium: Does the headline match how the reader would search? Is the first paragraph earning the read? Are there clear subheadings for scannability?
- Substack / Newsletter: Is the subject line a genuine hook (not a blog title)? Is there one CTA? Is the voice personal and specific — not broadcast?
- Operator response content: Is there any promotional language? Does it read as native to the platform? Does it directly answer the question asked?

**If this is a LinkedIn post:**
After channel fixes, check the strategy.
Reference `knowledge/08_linkedin_strategy.md`.
Is the strategy being executed correctly or did the ghost writer abandon it mid-post?
A hot take that walks itself back in paragraph 2 needs a rewrite, not a light edit.
A newsjack with a stale news angle needs a new hook, not a polish.
