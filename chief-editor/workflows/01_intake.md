# Step 1: Intake

Read the full input before doing anything else.

Identify all five before moving to diagnosis:

**Type**
Landing page section, blog post, Medium article, Substack post, newsletter, LinkedIn post, carousel, email, ad, operator response (Reddit, TripAdvisor, Quora, etc.).

**Channel / Touchpoint**
Where does this content live?

Wandar's content channels:
- LinkedIn (primary B2B channel)
- Landing page (conversion, ICP-only)
- Medium (authority + SEO/AEO)
- Substack (founder voice, warm subscribers)
- Newsletter (operator pipeline, warm leads)

Operator response content (drafted for operators to post on monitoring platforms):
- Reddit response, TripAdvisor reply, Quora answer, etc.

The channel determines which audience is reading it, what format rules apply, and what the success criteria are.

**Audience**
Who specifically is reading THIS piece on THIS channel?
Do not default to "luxury safari operators and DMCs" without thinking.

Examples:
- LinkedIn post (industry take on lead gen) → operators, DMC principals, adjacent founders, travel media
- LinkedIn post (hot take aimed at operator pain) → operators and DMCs only
- LinkedIn post (fundraising or market angle) → investors, founders, media
- Landing page → ICP only: luxury operators and DMCs actively evaluating Wandar
- Medium article → operators searching for solutions, travel industry professionals, investors
- Substack or newsletter → opted-in subscribers, warm operator leads
- Operator Reddit response → travelers seeking safari recommendations

If the audience is mixed or unclear: flag it and go to Step 2 (Clarify).

**Intent**
Inform, convert, earn shares, establish authority, rank in search, trigger comments, nurture relationship.

**Funnel stage**
Awareness, consideration, decision.

Do not begin Step 3 (Diagnose) until all five are clear.
If any are ambiguous, go to Step 2 (Clarify) first.
