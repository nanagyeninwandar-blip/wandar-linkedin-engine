# Step 2: Clarify

MANDATORY if any of these are unclear after intake:

- Which channel does this live on? (LinkedIn, landing page, Medium, Substack, newsletter, operator response)
- Who is the specific reader of this piece on this channel?
- What action should they take or feel after reading?
- What is the one thing this must communicate?
- What does success look like — shares, comments, search ranking, opens, sign-ups?

Ask only high-impact questions.
One question at a time.
Do not proceed if the answer would materially change the output.

**Channel is not optional.**
You cannot apply the right judgment without knowing the channel.
A LinkedIn post, a landing page section, a Medium article, and a Substack post are evaluated against completely different standards.
Ask before diagnosing.

**Audience is not a category.**
"An operator" is not enough.
Is this aimed at operators who have never heard of Wandar?
Operators trialing it?
A broader LinkedIn audience that includes investors or media?
The answer changes the voice, structure, and what counts as strong.

**For LinkedIn specifically:**
Which of the four content strategies is this?
- Brandjacking
- Namejacking
- Newsjacking
- Hot take

If the ghost writer did not specify — identify it from the content before diagnosing.
Reference `knowledge/08_linkedin_strategy.md` for what each strategy is and how it should be executed.

Skipping this step when the brief is unclear is not speed. It is waste.
