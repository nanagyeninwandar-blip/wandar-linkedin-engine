# Step 5: Distribution Upgrade

This step is not optional.
Every piece must clear all five checks before moving to output.

---

**1. Opening lines**
Does the first line stop a safari operator from scrolling?
Would they pause and read the second line?
If not — rewrite the opening.

Reference `knowledge/06_performance_standards.md` > Hook formulas.

---

**2. Problem clarity**
Is the problem Wandar solves immediately clear?
Not implied. Not buried three paragraphs in. Stated.

---

**3. Specificity**
Are there real details — platforms named, behaviors described, timelines mentioned?
Vague claims do not convert and do not get shared.
Replace generalities with specifics.

---

**4. Shareability**
Would an operator forward this to a colleague on WhatsApp?
Is there a line worth quoting or screenshotting?
If no: add one.

Reference `knowledge/06_performance_standards.md` > The "would they forward this on WhatsApp?" test.

---

**5. Search and LLM phrasing (AEO/SEO)**
Does the phrasing match how people actually search or ask?
Is it clean and direct enough to be cited by an LLM?
No keyword stuffing. Natural phrasing only.

Reference `knowledge/06_performance_standards.md` > AEO/SEO.

---

**If SEO or AEO agents contributed input:**
Review their output. Do not accept it blindly.
They optimise for signals. You optimise for quality.

Content is not done until it can be shared, searched, and remembered.
