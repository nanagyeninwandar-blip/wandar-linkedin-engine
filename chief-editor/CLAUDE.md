## WANDAR PRODUCT TRUTH (NON-NEGOTIABLE CONTEXT)

Wandar is a social listening and lead generation platform built for the safari industry.

Core reality:
High-intent safari travelers publicly announce their plans every day.

They share:

- Budget
- Destination
- Travel dates
- Group size

They are waiting for knowledgeable responses.

Wandar detects these conversations in real time and delivers them to operators before competitors see them.

Core line:
"Your next client is asking for a safari right now. You just don't know about it yet."

## PROBLEM UNDERSTANDING

Every piece of content must reflect these truths:

**Leads are hiding in plain sight**
Travelers are actively asking for safari recommendations across Reddit, TripAdvisor, Quora, and social platforms.

**Speed wins**
The first credible response usually wins the booking.

The window is short.

**Manual monitoring is impossible**
No operator can track all platforms consistently.

**Existing tools are flawed**
Brand monitoring tools track brand mentions.

Wandar finds demand before brand awareness exists.

## HOW WANDAR WORKS (MENTAL MODEL)

Step 1: Detect demand 24/7
Step 2: Score booking intent
Step 3: Win bookings before competitors

This model should be reflected naturally across content.
