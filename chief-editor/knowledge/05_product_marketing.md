# 5. Product Marketing

**Positioning:**
For luxury safari operators and DMCs who rely on referrals and manual outreach, Wandar is the only platform that finds high-intent safari travelers before they contact a competitor, by monitoring the platforms where demand is publicly expressed.

Unlike brand monitoring tools (which track mentions of your brand), Wandar finds demand before you exist in the traveler's consideration set.

**Messaging hierarchy:**

Primary: Safari travelers announce their plans publicly every day. Wandar finds them first.

Supporting:
- Wandar monitors 9 platforms in real time
- Intent scoring separates serious buyers from casual browsers
- Speed is the advantage — first credible response usually wins

Proof:
- Specific platform examples (Reddit threads, TripAdvisor forums, Quora questions)
- The reality that travelers share budget, dates, and destination openly

**ICP (who converts):**

Converts: Operators with established expertise who struggle to scale inbound. They already know their product is strong. Their problem is finding new demand outside referrals.

Does not convert: Operators who rely entirely on one OTA and are not looking to diversify. Operators who are brand-new with no product to sell.

**Competitive context:**

| Alternative | What it does | Why Wandar is different |
|-------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Brand monitoring tools (Mention, Brandwatch) | Tracks mentions of your brand | Wandar finds demand before the operator is mentioned |
| OTA lead gen (Booking.com, etc.) | Passes leads after traveler submits a form | Wandar finds intent before traveler reaches a booking platform |
| Google Ads | Captures active search | Wandar captures conversational intent on community platforms |
| Manual monitoring | Operator checks platforms manually | Impossible to scale, misses most activity |

**Key operator objections and how to address them:**

"I get enough leads from referrals."
Reality: Referrals are not scalable. Wandar adds a channel that doesn't depend on existing client relationships.

"I don't have time to respond to leads."
Reality: Wandar delivers pre-qualified, high-intent leads. Less noise than a generic lead gen tool.

"Safari travelers don't use Reddit."
Reality: They do. The posts are public. Show them one real example and the objection disappears.

**Proof points and credibility signals:**
- Specific platform examples with real post types
- The mechanics of intent scoring (not just "AI-powered")
- Founder understanding of the operator world (tone, not pitch)

**Operator vocabulary vs. language to avoid:**

Use: "bookings," "operators," "itinerary," "high season," "peak," "DMC," "inbound," "WhatsApp," "traveler"

Avoid: "pipeline," "leads pipeline," "funnel," "MQLs," "synergy," "leverage," "scalable solution," "platform ecosystem"
