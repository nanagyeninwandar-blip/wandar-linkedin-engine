# 2. Content Strategy

**Content pillars:**

| Pillar | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| Authority | Position Wandar and its operators as safari industry insiders |
| Demand capture | Attract operators actively searching for lead gen solutions |
| Conversion | Move operators from awareness to sign-up |
| Education | Explain how demand is expressed and why speed matters |

**Formats and their jobs:**

| Format | Job |
|--------|-----|
| Blog post | Authority + SEO + top-of-funnel awareness |
| Long-form guide | Deep authority + AEO + sustained search traffic |
| Platform page | Demand capture for specific platform searches |
| Landing page section | Conversion + clarity at the decision stage |
| Carousel | Shareability + platform reach + authority in digestible form |
| Email | Nurture + conversion + relationship |
| Social post | Reach + shares + community presence |
| Ad copy | Immediate attention + click intent |

**Funnel mapping:**

- Awareness: blog posts, social posts, guides, platform pages
- Consideration: guides, landing page sections, email sequences
- Decision: landing page CTAs, pricing page, case studies, direct outreach

**Repurposing logic:**
One strong piece becomes five.
Blog post → carousel slides → email newsletter → social pull quote → guide section

Start with the format that earns the most. Repurpose outward.

**What Wandar content must never do:**
- Chase vanity metrics (likes with no intent behind them)
- Attract travelers instead of operators
- Sound like generic SaaS marketing
- Publish lifestyle safari content that has nothing to do with lead generation

**Editorial standards by format:**

| Format | Length | CTA type |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Blog post | 800-1,500 words | Soft (read more, related guide) or sign-up |
| Long-form guide | 2,000-4,000 words | Email capture or early access |
| Platform page | 600-1,000 words | Sign-up or demo |
| Landing page section | 50-200 words | Scroll or sign-up |
| Carousel | 5-10 slides, 15-40 words per slide | Save, share, follow |
| Email | 150-400 words | One clear CTA |
| Social post | 50-200 words | Engagement or link click |
| Ad copy | 15-50 words | Click |
