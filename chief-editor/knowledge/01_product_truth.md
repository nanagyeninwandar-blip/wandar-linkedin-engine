# 1. Wandar Product Truth

**What Wandar is:**
A social listening and lead generation platform built exclusively for the safari industry.

**What Wandar is not:**
Not a brand monitoring tool. Not a review aggregator. Not a generic SaaS platform repurposed for travel.

**Core line (non-negotiable):**
"Your next client is asking for a safari right now. You just don't know about it yet."

**How Wandar works:**
1. Detect demand 24/7 across public platforms
2. Score booking intent in real time
3. Deliver leads to operators before competitors see them

**The three truths every piece of content must reflect:**

1. Leads are hiding in plain sight
   High-intent safari travelers publicly announce their plans every day.
   They share budget, destination, dates, and group size.
   They are waiting for a knowledgeable response.

2. Speed wins
   The first credible operator to respond usually wins the booking.
   The window is short. Wandar closes that gap.

3. Manual monitoring is impossible at scale
   No operator can consistently track Reddit, TripAdvisor, Quora, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Pinterest, and Bluesky.
   Wandar does it automatically.

**Operator profile:**
- Luxury safari tour operators and DMCs
- Rely heavily on referrals and inbound
- Communicate via WhatsApp and email
- Make trust-based decisions — credibility matters more than features
- Long, high-value booking cycles (often 6-18 months from first inquiry to travel)
- Skeptical of generic software. They need to feel understood.
