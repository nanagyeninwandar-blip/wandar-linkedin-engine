# 3. Content Distribution Channels

Wandar's content lives on five channels. Every piece the Chief Content Editor receives will belong to one of these.

---

**LinkedIn** — Primary B2B content channel.

The most important channel for reaching safari operators, DMCs, investors, and travel industry professionals. Ghost writers will produce the highest volume of content here. Standards must be the highest here.

Full judgment criteria: `knowledge/09_channel_intelligence.md`
Full strategy guidance: `knowledge/08_linkedin_strategy.md`

---

**Landing Page** — Conversion channel. ICP-only audience.

Every section of the landing page is aimed at one reader: a luxury safari operator or DMC principal who is evaluating whether to use Wandar. They are at consideration or decision stage. They are skeptical of software. Every word must earn its place.

Full judgment criteria: `knowledge/09_channel_intelligence.md`

---

**Medium** — Authority and SEO/AEO channel.

Long-form content that earns search traffic and LLM citation. Audience is broader than LinkedIn — includes operators searching for solutions, travel industry professionals, investors, and media. Medium is where Wandar builds its written authority record.

Full judgment criteria: `knowledge/09_channel_intelligence.md`

---

**Substack** — Founder voice channel. Warm subscriber audience.

Operators and industry people who have opted in. They know Wandar exists. The tone is more personal and direct than Medium. Substack builds the relationship between Wandar and the operator community.

Full judgment criteria: `knowledge/09_channel_intelligence.md`

---

**Newsletter (planned)** — Operator pipeline channel. Warm leads.

Operators in Wandar's pipeline and broader subscriber list. Same editorial standards as Substack until the newsletter is fully operational. When live: full segmentation capability means content can be targeted to specific operator types.

Full judgment criteria: `knowledge/09_channel_intelligence.md`

---

**Note on the 9 monitoring platforms:**
Reddit, TripAdvisor, Quora, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Pinterest, and Bluesky are the platforms Wandar's product monitors for safari lead intelligence. They are not Wandar's own content distribution channels.

Exception: operator response content. Ghost writers may draft responses for operators to post on these platforms (e.g., a Reddit reply to a traveler asking for safari recommendations). That content may be submitted to the Chief Content Editor for review. Judgment criteria for operator response content: `knowledge/04_monitoring_platform_intelligence.md`
