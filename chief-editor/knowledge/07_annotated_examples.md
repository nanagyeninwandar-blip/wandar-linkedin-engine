# 7. Annotated Examples

**Example 1 — Light edit**

Input:
"Wandar is a powerful social listening platform that helps safari operators and travel businesses identify and engage with potential clients across multiple digital channels, enabling them to respond to high-intent travelers in a timely manner."

Diagnosis:
- Generic (could apply to any industry) [flag 1]
- AI-sounding (passive, over-qualified) [flag 2]

Output:
"Safari travelers post their plans publicly every day — budget, dates, destination, group size. Wandar finds those posts and delivers them to operators before anyone else responds."

What made it stronger:
Specificity replaced vagueness. The traveler behavior is named. The operator benefit is direct.

---

**Example 2 — Heavy rewrite**

Input:
"In today's competitive safari market, it's more important than ever for operators to leverage cutting-edge technology to stay ahead of the competition. Wandar provides a comprehensive solution for monitoring online conversations and identifying potential leads across various social media platforms, helping operators to maximize their business potential."

Diagnosis:
- Generic [flag 1]
- AI-sounding [flag 2]
- Forgettable [flag 3]
- No specificity [flag 4]
- No hook [flag 5]

Output:
"Right now, someone is asking Reddit which Tanzania operator to book.
They've named their budget. They've said when they're traveling.
They're waiting for a reply.
Most operators will never see that post.
Wandar finds it and puts it in front of you."

What made it stronger:
Specific scenario. Present tense creates urgency. Operator pain is named without being stated as a corporate problem.

---

**Example 3 — Rejection**

Input:
"Wandar is a revolutionary AI-powered platform that transforms the way safari operators connect with their target audience by utilizing advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze sentiment across social media ecosystems, providing actionable insights that drive measurable ROI and sustainable competitive advantage."

Why it cannot be polished:
Every phrase is generic SaaS language. There is no safari-specific content. There is no human voice. There is no product truth. Polishing this sentence by sentence would still produce weak copy because the thinking behind it is wrong.

What a rebuild needs:
Start with a specific traveler behavior. Name the platforms. Name the operator pain. Remove every piece of SaaS vocabulary. Write for a founder who respects safari operators, not a marketer who is afraid to be specific.

What made the rejection right:
Polishing it would have taken longer than rebuilding it and produced worse output.
