# 6. Performance Standards

**Anatomy of a high-performing piece in this niche:**

1. Hook — stops the skim
2. Insight — one thing they didn't know or hadn't articulated
3. Proof — specific, grounded evidence
4. CTA — one clear next action

Every piece needs all four, in that order.

**What triggers shares in the safari operator niche:**

- Specificity that signals insider knowledge ("On any given day, there are 40-60 posts on Reddit from travelers with confirmed safari budgets over $5,000")
- Something that articulates a frustration they've felt but couldn't name
- A surprising stat or behavior they can cite in conversation
- The "finally someone gets it" feeling

**Hook formulas that work:**

| Formula | Example |
|---------|---------|
| Surprising fact | "Every day, safari travelers post their exact budget and travel window on Reddit. Most operators never see it." |
| Direct address | "You have a client planning a $15,000 Tanzania safari right now. They just don't know your name yet." |
| Bold claim | "The first operator to respond to a high-intent inquiry usually wins the booking. Not the best operator. The fastest." |
| Question that creates tension | "How many high-intent safari inquiries did your competitors respond to this week that you never saw?" |

**What kills reach:**

- Generic openers ("In today's competitive landscape...")
- AI phrasing ("It's important to note that...", "In conclusion...", "By leveraging...")
- Passive voice throughout
- Vague claims with no specifics
- Em dashes (signals AI-generated to trained readers)
- Opening with the company name ("Wandar is a platform that...")

**AEO/SEO — writing to be cited and found:**

Write answers, not just content.
Structure responses so an LLM can extract a clean answer.
Use the exact phrasing travelers and operators use when searching.

Good: "Safari travelers on Reddit typically post their budget, destination, travel dates, and group size publicly."
Weak: "Many people use social platforms to discuss travel plans."

Natural phrasing wins over keyword insertion.

**The "would they forward this on WhatsApp?" test:**
Paste the strongest line out of context. Read it cold.
Does it make sense? Is it worth sending?
If not — rewrite that line until it does.

**Benchmark expectations by format:**

| Format | Success signal |
|--------|---------------|
| Blog post | Organic search traffic within 60-90 days, shares from operators |
| Guide | Featured snippet, LLM citation, email sign-ups |
| Platform page | Search traffic for platform-specific queries |
| Carousel | Saves, shares, profile follows |
| Email | Open rate above industry average, replies from operators |
| Social post | Saves and shares over likes |
| Ad | Click-through, not impressions |
